# Bonus flight artwork

Generated with the built-in image generation tool. Original PNGs were copied without raster editing to `static/bonuses/`. No folder named `generated` is used in the app.

## storm-run.png

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: wide landscape illustration for a Dragon Flight bonus route card. A dramatic alpine mountain pass with towering natural dark granite cliffs, swirling storm clouds, fine rain and a few distant lightning veins illuminating a clear central flying corridor. Premium hand-painted naturalistic fantasy game background, softly painted detailed textures, deep blue teal palette with pale silver highlights, atmospheric depth. Wide 3:2 composition, recognizable focal scene in upper two thirds, restrained darker lower third for HTML text overlay. Environment only, no birds, dragons, people, logos, text, letters, numbers, UI, borders or coins. Fully painted opaque background.
