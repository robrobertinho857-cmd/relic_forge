## Clear day

```text
Use case: stylized-concept.
Asset type: production landscape background for Dragon Flight, a side-view flying-creature game.
Generate one single full-bleed wide 16:9 background image, ideally 1920 x 1080 pixels, with no panel divisions.
Scene: a grounded natural alpine mountain valley, distant layered blue-gray mountain ridges, a winding narrow river low in the valley, pine forest clustered along the lower corners and slopes, pale distant snow on the highest peaks. Elevated side-view landscape, looking horizontally across the valley, not a first-person road view. A premium hand-painted 2D game environment, polished digital landscape illustration with natural shapes, soft atmospheric depth and broad readable color masses. Detailed enough to feel rich, restrained enough to support moving game sprites.
Composition: generous open pale-blue sky in the upper half; far mountains below the upper third; broad quiet middle band with softened distant terrain for a flying sprite to remain readable; foreground vegetation only in the bottom 20 percent and lower corners. Keep important terrain away from outer edges to allow both wide desktop and center-cropped mobile views. Clean edge-to-edge artwork.
Lighting: CLEAR DAY, calm bright natural daylight, soft scattered white clouds, subtle warm sun illumination, readable green forest, muted blue water. No prominent sun disk. Consistent moderate contrast, no harsh lens effects.
Constraints: BACKGROUND ONLY. No birds, no dragons, no animals, no humans, no silhouettes of creatures, no buildings, no architecture, no gates, no floating objects, no collectibles, no magic, no fantasy ruins, no lava, no text, no letters, no logo, no watermark, no border, no UI. Not a screenshot, not a collage.
```
