# Full mountain silhouettes

Four replacement rock assets generated with the built-in image generation tool. Saved without raster editing in `static/terrain/natural/` as `rock-upper-rounded.png`, `rock-lower-rounded.png`, and their `-snow` variants. Snow edits reference their corresponding base PNG and share that alpha mask at display time.

## rock-upper

Use case: stylized-concept. A single natural alpine cliff overhang attached to the TOP canvas edge, tapering down to a rounded uneven underside close to the BOTTOM edge. Transparent isolated game obstacle sprite, portrait 2:3. Weathered rounded gray granite ledges with small patches of moss, hand-painted naturalistic mountain game art, cool blue-gray shadows and neutral daylight. CRITICAL: the entire LEFT and RIGHT contours must be fully visible, with a generous truly transparent margin at least 12 percent canvas width along BOTH sides, for the FULL height. Never touch left or right canvas edges, even at the attached base. No vertical rectangular cutoffs, no straight cut sides, no pointed crystal. Only the top attachment may continue outside the canvas. Center the solid free tip horizontally. One connected formation, no floating pieces. Actual alpha transparency outside the rock; NO painted background, checkerboard, outer glow, haze, floor shadow, sky, scenery, text, creatures, UI or snow.
