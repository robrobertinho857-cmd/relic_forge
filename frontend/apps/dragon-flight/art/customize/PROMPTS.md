# Customize button artwork

Twelve illustrations generated with the built-in image_gen tool and saved in `static/customize/generated/`.

Time and weather use nine dedicated symbols; Clear shares the Day sun. Glide, Boost and Dive use three bird illustrations solely to explain the takeoff animation. These menu pictures do not replace the in-game creature sprites.

## glide.png

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: small square illustrated selection-button artwork for a mountain flying game. A small elegant golden bird viewed from the side flying smoothly from left to right, long wings held level, a gentle horizontal ribbon of pale air behind it. Calm level flight. Premium hand-painted game illustration, simple readable silhouette, warm cream and amber feathers with subtle natural detail, muted alpine blue ridges far behind and deep midnight teal sky. Center the whole bird and its short trail within the middle 65 percent of the square so it stays readable at 60 pixels wide. Restrained atmospheric depth, soft rim lighting, pleasing realistic painterly finish. Full bleed opaque square composition. No frame, badge, circle, text, letters, numbers, UI, logo, watermark or extra birds. This is one illustration, not a sheet of variants.
