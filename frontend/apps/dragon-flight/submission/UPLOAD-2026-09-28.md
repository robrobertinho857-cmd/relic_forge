## Game description

Dragon Flight takes players through mountain valleys, forest passes and high skies. Choose a bird, a base bet and one of three risk modes, or enter the Storm Run and Summit Expedition bonus flights. Each independent server-selected result unfolds as a flight ending in a landing or crash. Creature, scenery and playback settings affect presentation only; there is no timing-based survival or cashout.
